# Quick Preview privacy policy

Updated 25 November 2026.

Quick Preview previews SoundCloud tracks when you hover over their play buttons.

## What the extension handles

The extension reads SoundCloud track links and waveform images and responds to hover and click events to provide previews. It temporarily uses the current page URL, selected track URL and playback state. It does not keep a browsing-history or activity log.

Your preferred sample count and sample duration are saved locally in your browser. Removing the extension removes its saved preferences.

## SoundCloud playback

To play a preview, the extension sends the selected track URL to SoundCloud's embedded player at w.soundcloud.com. Loading that player makes network requests to SoundCloud, which receives normal connection information such as your IP address and may use cookies under its own [privacy policy](https://soundcloud.com/pages/privacy).

## Use and sharing

The extension has no developer-operated backend, analytics or advertising tracking. We do not sell user data. Information is used only to provide track previews and save preferences, not for advertising, unrelated purposes, creditworthiness or lending. Any transfer to SoundCloud is solely to provide the requested playback.

Quick Preview's use of user data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
